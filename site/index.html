Villablino is a mountain town in north-west Spain, in the province of León, region of Castile and León. It is the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, and home to 7,585 people (INE, 2025). If you are looking for a quiet life surrounded by nature, without giving up fast internet, healthcare or good connections, this guide tells you exactly what to expect.

Location

Where is Villablino?

Villablino sits in the Cantabrian Mountains, between León and Asturias. It is a real town with shops, cafés, supermarkets and public services, not an isolated village.

Nature

A UNESCO Biosphere Reserve on your doorstep

The Laciana Valley covers around 22,000 hectares of protected mountains, forests and rivers. Peaks rise above 2,000 metres, and the valley has some of the finest birch forests in the Iberian Peninsula.

It is also a stronghold of two of Spain's rarest animals: the brown bear and the Cantabrian capercaillie. They live in the surrounding mountains, not in town, so you share the valley with wild nature while living in a fully serviced town.

Remote work

Fibre internet in the mountains

Villablino has fibre optic internet in town, plus 4G and 5G mobile coverage. Your office, with a view of the mountains. For homes in the smaller villages of the valley, we check the connection at the exact address before you decide.

Everyday life

Everything you need, close to home

The town has a health centre with an emergency service, pharmacies, supermarkets, banks with ATMs and a post office. The main reference hospital for the area is Hospital El Bierzo, in Ponferrada.

Heritage

Stone, slate and history

The villages around Villablino keep their traditional mountain architecture: stone churches, slate roofs and green meadows climbing up the hillsides.

Work and business

Who is Villablino for?

Villablino suits people who work remotely, retirees, and professionals with a job in the area such as healthcare, education or public services. For entrepreneurs, the Spanish government has announced new grant lines for business projects in former mining regions such as Laciana, expected from late 2026.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Villablino a good place to live?

Yes, if you want mountain nature and a quiet pace of life with essential services close by: fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, shops, banks, pharmacies and a cultural centre.

Can I work remotely from Villablino?

Yes. The town has fibre optic internet plus 4G and 5G mobile coverage.

How far is Villablino from an airport?

León Airport is 98 km away (about 1 h 20) and Asturias Airport is 153 km away (about 1 h 51), with daily flights to Madrid.

How far is Villablino from the beach?

The Atlantic coast of Asturias (Cudillero) is about 110 km away, roughly 2 hours by car.

Are there brown bears near Villablino?

Yes. The Laciana Valley is one of the brown bear's strongholds in Spain. Bears live in the surrounding mountains, not in town.
