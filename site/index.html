FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

How does your home search work?

Tell us what you need, we send you a shortlist with area guides, the owner shows you the home in person or by video call, and we negotiate the price for you.

How much do you charge?

You only pay if you buy. Our fee comes out of the discount we negotiate, and we explain the exact terms in writing before you see any property.

Can I buy without travelling to Spain?

Yes. You can view by video call and sign through a power of attorney. You will need an NIE, the tax number every foreign buyer needs.
