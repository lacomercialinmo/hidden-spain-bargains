Living in Ponferrada

The Templar city of El Bierzo: a green valley of vineyards, Roman gold mines and the Camino de Santiago, with city services and very low prices.

Why Ponferrada stands out

  • The Knights Templar held the town until 1312, and the castle covers about 8,000 m².
  • Las Médulas, the Roman gold mines and a UNESCO World Heritage Site, are a short drive away.
  • El Bierzo is a wine region in its own right, with the Mencía grape and a mild climate for north-west Spain.

Homes and prices in Ponferrada

Flats in Ponferrada cost about €1,071 per m² and houses about €745 per m² (October 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €80,000. That is 62% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

Ponferrada has a railway station on the line between León and Galicia, and the A-6 motorway links it with Madrid and A Coruña. León is about 110 km away.

Everyday life

It is the capital of El Bierzo, with a hospital, schools, shopping centres and a university campus. Pilgrims on the Camino de Santiago pass through the old town all year.

Who Ponferrada suits

History lovers, wine lovers and families who want city services, mountains and vineyards within twenty minutes.
