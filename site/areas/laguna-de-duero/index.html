Living in Laguna de Duero

Not the lowest-priced town on our list, but one of the best for quality of life: pine woods, a lagoon and Valladolid ten minutes away.

Why Laguna de Duero stands out

  • A green town of pine woods, with its own lagoon and walking paths.
  • Modern housing with gardens and community pools is common.
  • Valladolid city, with its high-speed trains to Madrid, is next door.

Homes and prices in Laguna de Duero

Flats in Laguna de Duero cost about €1,876 per m² and houses about €1,683 per m² (October 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €141,000. That is 33% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

Laguna is about 8 km from central Valladolid, with frequent buses. Valladolid has high-speed trains to Madrid.

Everyday life

It has health centres, many schools, sports facilities and shops. Families choose it for space and greenery close to a big city.

Who Laguna de Duero suits

Families and professionals who want city jobs and services with a greener, calmer home life.
