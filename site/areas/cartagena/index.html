Living in Cartagena

A 3,000-year-old port city on the Mediterranean, with a Roman theatre in the old town and wild beaches nearby.

CoastHeritageSun

Why Cartagena stands out

  • Its Roman theatre, rediscovered in the 1980s, is now one of the great sights of south-east Spain.
  • The old town has fine Modernist buildings and a busy marina.
  • The Mar Menor and the wild coves of Calblanque regional park are close by.

Homes and prices in Cartagena

Flats in Cartagena cost about €1,842 per m² and houses about €1,692 per m² (October 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €138,000. That is 34% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

Cartagena is linked by motorway with Murcia, and Murcia's international airport is close.

Everyday life

The city has hospitals, schools, a university and a naval base, and all the services of a large town by the sea.

Who Cartagena suits

Sea lovers and history lovers who want the Mediterranean at prices below the Costa Blanca and Costa del Sol.
