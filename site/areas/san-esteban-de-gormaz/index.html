Living in San Esteban de Gormaz

A quiet town on the Duero with two of the oldest Romanesque porticoed churches in Spain, in the Soria part of Ribera del Duero.

Why San Esteban de Gormaz stands out

  • Its churches of San Miguel and Nuestra Señora del Rivero have some of the earliest Romanesque porticoes in Spain.
  • The town stands on the Duero, with a medieval bridge and the ruins of its castle above.
  • It is on the route of the Cid, and part of the Ribera del Duero wine area.

Homes and prices in San Esteban de Gormaz

Flats in San Esteban de Gormaz cost about €1,162 per m² and houses about €705 per m² (September 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €87,000. That is 58% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

San Esteban is on the N-122 along the Duero. Aranda de Duero and El Burgo de Osma are each a short drive away.

Everyday life

The town has a health centre, schools, shops and bars. Bigger services are in Aranda de Duero and Soria.

Who San Esteban de Gormaz suits

People who want history, wine and silence, and a house for a very modest budget.
