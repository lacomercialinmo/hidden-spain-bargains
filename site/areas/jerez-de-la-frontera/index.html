Living in Jerez de la Frontera

The city of sherry, horses and flamenco, with its own airport and the beaches of Cádiz half an hour away.

Why Jerez de la Frontera stands out

  • Sherry comes from here, and many historic bodegas can be visited.
  • Home of the Royal Andalusian School of Equestrian Art and a living flamenco tradition.
  • The beaches of the Bay of Cádiz are a short drive away.

Homes and prices in Jerez de la Frontera

Flats in Jerez de la Frontera cost about €1,917 per m² and houses about €1,821 per m² (October 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €144,000. That is 31% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

Jerez has its own airport and a railway station on the line between Seville and Cádiz.

Everyday life

As a large city, Jerez has hospitals, schools, a university campus and every kind of shop.

Who Jerez de la Frontera suits

People who want the Andalusian dream, with sun, culture and beaches, at prices below the coast.
