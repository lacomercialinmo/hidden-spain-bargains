Living in Lugo

A city still fully ringed by its Roman walls, which you can walk all the way round, in green inland Galicia.

Why Lugo stands out

  • Its Roman walls are complete and are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • The Camino Primitivo, the oldest Way to Santiago, passes through the city.
  • A great tapas tradition: many bars serve a free tapa with each drink.

Homes and prices in Lugo

Flats in Lugo cost about €1,847 per m² and houses about €1,116 per m² (October 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €139,000. That is 34% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

Lugo is linked by motorway with A Coruña and Santiago, and by the A-6 with Madrid.

Everyday life

As a provincial capital, Lugo has a hospital, a university campus, schools and markets. The pace is calm and the countryside is close.

Who Lugo suits

History lovers and families who want a safe, walkable city with green countryside all around.
