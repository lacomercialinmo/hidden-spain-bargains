Area guides

Places in Spain most buyers never hear about

Beautiful towns and small cities with hospitals, schools and trains, where a flat costs a fraction of the Spanish average of €2,787 per m². Each guide shows current prices, how to get around and who the place suits.

Castile and León · Galicia and Asturias · Madrid region and Castilla-La Mancha · Murcia and the Valencia region · Extremadura and Andalusia · Prices checked November 2026

Castile and León

LeónVillablino & Laciana ValleyHomes around €539/m² (median, October 2026)

A mountain town in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, with fibre internet and some of the lowest home prices in Spain.

BurgosAranda de DueroFlats from about €1,702/m²

The capital of the Ribera del Duero wine region, with miles of medieval cellars under its streets and the A-1 motorway to Madrid.

ÁvilaArévaloFlats from about €1,052/m²

A Mudéjar brick town between Ávila, Segovia and Valladolid, where Isabella of Castile grew up.

LeónAstorgaFlats from about €1,096/m²

A Roman town on two pilgrim routes, with a palace by Gaudí, a chocolate tradition and houses at some of the lowest prices in the region.

LeónBembibreFlats from about €696/m²

A friendly market town in El Bierzo where flats cost a quarter of the Spanish average, twenty minutes from Ponferrada.

ValladolidLaguna de DueroFlats from about €1,876/m²

Not the lowest-priced town on our list, but one of the best for quality of life: pine woods, a lagoon and Valladolid ten minutes away.

ValladolidMedina de RiosecoFlats from about €1,126/m²

The 'city of the admirals' in the Tierra de Campos: a porticoed high street, grand churches and the end of the Canal de Castilla.

ValladolidMedina del CampoFlats from about €1,148/m²

A royal market town with a great brick castle, a high-speed rail station and the vineyards of Rueda all around.

SegoviaNava de la AsunciónFlats from about €757/m²

A village in Segovia's pine forests, close to the castle of Coca, where a house with a garden costs less than a city parking space elsewhere.

PalenciaPalenciaFlats from about €1,812/m²

A calm, walkable cathedral city on the Carrión river, with high-speed trains to Madrid and prices far below the Spanish average.

ValladolidPeñafielFlats from about €1,047/m²

The heart of Ribera del Duero wine country, under a castle shaped like a ship that houses the provincial wine museum.

LeónPonferradaFlats from about €1,071/m²

The Templar city of El Bierzo: a green valley of vineyards, Roman gold mines and the Camino de Santiago, with city services and very low prices.

SoriaSan Esteban de GormazFlats from about €1,162/m²

A quiet town on the Duero with two of the oldest Romanesque porticoed churches in Spain, in the Soria part of Ribera del Duero.

SoriaSoriaFlats from about €2,214/m²

Spain's quietest provincial capital: Romanesque churches, the Duero river, pine forests and the poet Antonio Machado's streets.

PalenciaVenta de BañosFlats from about €1,022/m²

A small town built around one of northern Spain's best-known railway junctions, ten minutes from Palencia and close to Valladolid.

ZamoraZamoraFlats from about €1,581/m²

A golden-stone city on the Duero river, famous for its Romanesque churches and an hour's drive from the Portuguese border.

ÁvilaÁvilaFlats from about €1,752/m²

A city inside complete medieval walls, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, within easy reach of Madrid by train.

Galicia and Asturias

AsturiasAvilésFlats from about €1,766/m²

A historic Asturian town with porticoed streets, the Niemeyer Centre on its estuary and beaches and an airport close by.

A CoruñaFerrolFlats from about €1,318/m²

A naval city on the Galician coast, with Modernist streets, wild Atlantic beaches and the start of the English Way to Santiago.

LugoLugoFlats from about €1,847/m²

A city still fully ringed by its Roman walls, which you can walk all the way round, in green inland Galicia.

OurenseOurenseFlats from about €1,938/m²

A Galician city of hot springs, where you can bathe in free thermal pools along the Miño river, with high-speed trains to Madrid.

PontevedraPontevedraFlats from about €2,599/m²

The small Galician city famous for giving its centre back to pedestrians, at the heart of the Rías Baixas and Albariño country.

Madrid region and Castilla-La Mancha

MadridPerales de TajuñaFlats from about €1,922/m²

A village in the green Tajuña valley, inside the Madrid region, with a greenway for walking and cycling and Madrid about 45 km away.

Ciudad RealPuertollanoFlats from about €772/m²

A town with a high-speed station on the Madrid–Seville line and some of the lowest flat prices of any town its size in Spain.

CuencaTarancónFlats from about €1,396/m²

A La Mancha town on the A-3 motorway, about 80 km from Madrid, with a Roman city and a great monastery nearby.

Murcia and the Valencia region

MurciaCartagenaFlats from about €1,842/m²

A 3,000-year-old port city on the Mediterranean, with a Roman theatre in the old town and wild beaches nearby.

AlicanteEldaFlats from about €1,093/m²

Spain's shoe-making capital in the Vinalopó valley, about 40 minutes from Alicante, its beaches and its airport.

MurciaMurciaFlats from about €1,789/m²

A sunny, lively city with a baroque cathedral, orange and lemon orchards all around and the Mediterranean about 45 minutes away.

ValenciaOntinyentFlats from about €1,255/m²

A textile town in the green Vall d'Albaida, with natural river pools and one of the biggest Moors and Christians festivals in the region.

ValenciaXàtivaFlats from about €1,389/m²

The town of the Borgia popes, under a great hilltop castle, with commuter trains to Valencia.

Extremadura and Andalusia

BadajozAlmendralejoFlats from about €912/m²

The wine town of Extremadura, in the vineyards of Tierra de Barros, about 25 minutes from Roman Mérida.

BadajozBadajozFlats from about €1,843/m²

A sunny border city on the Guadiana river, with a great Moorish citadel and Portugal across the bridge.

CórdobaCórdobaFlats from about €1,995/m²

One of the world's great historic cities, home of the Mosque-Cathedral, with high-speed trains to Madrid and Seville.

HuelvaHuelvaFlats from about €1,723/m²

The Andalusian city where Columbus's story begins, between marshland nature reserves and long Atlantic beaches.

JaénJaénFlats from about €1,719/m²

The capital of the world's largest olive oil region, under a hilltop castle, with a great Renaissance cathedral.

CádizJerez de la FronteraFlats from about €1,917/m²

The city of sherry, horses and flamenco, with its own airport and the beaches of Cádiz half an hour away.

JaénLa CarolinaFlats from about €623/m²

A planned 18th-century town at the gateway to Andalusia, on the A-4 motorway near the Despeñaperros pass.

HuelvaLepeFlats from about €2,132/m²

The Costa de la Luz without the crowds: long Atlantic beaches at La Antilla, strawberry fields, and Portugal half an hour away.

JaénLinaresFlats from about €928/m²

A mining city in Jaén province, birthplace of the guitarist Andrés Segovia, with flats at about a third of the Spanish average.

BadajozMéridaFlats from about €1,323/m²

The capital of Extremadura, where you can live next to a Roman theatre that still stages plays every summer.

CórdobaPeñarroya-PueblonuevoFlats from about €644/m²

A former mining town in the Guadiato valley, in the north of Córdoba province, with some of the lowest home prices in Andalusia.

JaénÚbedaFlats from about €1,210/m²

A Renaissance jewel among the olive groves of Jaén, a UNESCO World Heritage Site together with nearby Baeza.
