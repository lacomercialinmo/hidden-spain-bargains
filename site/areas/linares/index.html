Living in Linares

A mining city in Jaén province, birthplace of the guitarist Andrés Segovia, with flats at about a third of the Spanish average.

Why Linares stands out

  • The classical guitarist Andrés Segovia was born here.
  • A rich mining heritage and grand buildings from its boom years.
  • Úbeda and Baeza, both UNESCO World Heritage, are close by.

Homes and prices in Linares

Flats in Linares cost about €928 per m² and houses about €882 per m² (October 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €70,000. That is 67% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

Linares is on the A-4 corridor between Madrid and Andalusia, with the Linares-Baeza railway station.

Everyday life

It has a hospital, schools, shops and markets, with the services of a mid-sized city.

Who Linares suits

Buyers on a budget who want a real city with services, near two UNESCO towns.
