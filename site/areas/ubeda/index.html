Living in Úbeda

A Renaissance jewel among the olive groves of Jaén, a UNESCO World Heritage Site together with nearby Baeza.

Why Úbeda stands out

  • The Renaissance monuments of Úbeda and Baeza are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Golden-stone palaces and squares, with very few crowds.
  • Endless olive groves and the Cazorla mountains nearby.

Homes and prices in Úbeda

Flats in Úbeda cost about €1,210 per m² and houses about €920 per m² (October 2026, RealAdvisor). A 75 m² flat therefore works out at around €91,000. That is 57% below the Spanish average for flats, €2,787 per m².

These are averages. Homes that need work, or that have been on the market for a while, often sell well below them, and that is where our negotiation makes the difference.

Getting around

Úbeda is linked by road with Jaén, Linares and the A-4 motorway, and the Linares-Baeza station is nearby.

Everyday life

The town has a hospital, schools and shops, and a calm, safe atmosphere.

Who Úbeda suits

History lovers and retirees who want beauty and calm in the Andalusian countryside.
