Trade Canadian winters for Spanish ones

You don't want to give up winter, just the brutal part of it. In Villablino, in the mountains of north-west Spain, the climate has average highs of about 7°C in January and 28°C in July, with drier summers (approximate model averages).

Snow you can enjoy

Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from Villablino. You can ski in the morning and be home for lunch.

Summers that are actually pleasant

At about 1,015 m above sea level, Villablino stays fresher than the rest of Spain in July and August.

Prefer the sea?

On the Mediterranean coast, Castellón has January highs of about 14°C, August highs of about 31°C and around 444 mm of rain in a normal year. Read our Toronto-to-Castellón guide.

Where we would start looking: Villablino

Villablino is a mountain town in León, north-west Spain, and the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares. It has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), sits at about 1,015 m above sea level, and has fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, supermarkets and banks. León Airport is 98 km away and Asturias Airport 153 km, and Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from town.

Homes here cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026), against €2,639/m² (second-hand homes, Q4 2025) for Spain as a whole. See the homes we have selected or read our full guide to Villablino.
