Retiring to Spain on a UK State Pension

Retiring to Spain is still very possible after Brexit. The uk state pension is paid in spain and uprated every year, and UK State Pension recipients living in Spain may get Spanish state healthcare paid by the UK through an S1 form.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 6 min read

Step 1: the non-lucrative visa

This is the visa for people who will not work in Spain. You need €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026 (400% of IPREM), plus €7,200 for each family member; no work allowed in Spain. A State Pension on its own is often not enough, but most retirees combine it with a workplace or private pension, or savings.

Step 2: healthcare with the S1

Uk state pension recipients living in spain may get spanish state healthcare paid by the uk through an s1 form. Before you reach State Pension age, plan for private health insurance.

Step 3: a home with no mortgage

Retiring without rent or a mortgage changes everything. With homes in Villablino at around €539/m² (median, October 2026), many people can buy outright with the money from selling a UK home, and keep the rest as savings.

Why a mountain town and not the Costas?

Green valleys, four seasons, stone villages and no tourist crowds. Life is quiet, but the town has a health centre, pharmacies and shops within walking distance.

Where we would start looking: Villablino

Villablino is a mountain town in León, north-west Spain, and the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares. It has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), sits at about 1,015 m above sea level, and has fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, supermarkets and banks. León Airport is 98 km away and Asturias Airport 153 km, and Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from town.

Homes here cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026), against €2,639/m² (second-hand homes, Q4 2025) for Spain as a whole. See the homes we have selected or read our full guide to Villablino.
