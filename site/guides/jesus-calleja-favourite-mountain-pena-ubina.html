Jesús Calleja's favourite mountain is not in Alaska. It is Peña Ubiña

Jesús Calleja is Spain's best-known adventurer and TV explorer, and he has climbed Everest. Asked about his favourite mountain, he did not pick the Himalaya or Alaska. He picked Peña Ubiña (2,417 m, the highest summit of the Ubiñas massif), on the border between León and Asturias.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 4 min read

The story: on 24 November 2026 the Spanish newspaper OKDIARIO reported that Calleja calls Peña Ubiña his favourite mountain: "No es Alaska, mi montaña favorita es espectacular y maravillosa" ("It's not Alaska, my favourite mountain is spectacular and wonderful"). According to the report, it is where he started climbing as a young man, where his dream of the Himalaya began, and where he still returns after his big expeditions.

Where Peña Ubiña is

Peña Ubiña rises in the Cantabrian Mountains, on the watershed between León and Asturias. On the Asturian side it sits in the Las Ubiñas-La Mesa Natural Park, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2012, covering about 45,000 hectares in the Asturian municipalities of Lena, Quirós and Teverga. On the León side, the classic climb starts in the village of Torrebarrio, in Babia, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2004, about 38,000 hectares across the municipalities of Cabrillanes and San Emiliano.

Can you climb it?

Yes, but it is a serious day out. The route from Torrebarrio is about 10.5 km there and back with around 1,175 m of climbing, roughly 7 hours, rated medium-hard. In summer it has no technical sections, but in winter snow, ice and wind make it a mountaineering climb, so check the forecast and go with a guide if you are not experienced. If you just want the views, the walks through the valleys and high pastures below the peak are easy and beautiful.

Why this matters if you are thinking of moving here

Babia is the valley next to the Laciana Valley, where Villablino is. That puts one of Spain's most loved mountains a short drive from a real town with fibre internet, a health centre, schools and shops, where homes cost around €545/m² (median, October 2026). Brown bears, golden eagles, the ski slopes at Leitariegos and several Biosphere Reserves are all on the doorstep.

Living here, not just visiting

Villablino sits at about 1,015 m in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 23,000 hectares, with trails from every village and Leitariegos ski resort about 15 km away. It is a real town of 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), with fibre internet and services, and homes around €545/m² (median, October 2026). Frequently asked questions

Who is Jesús Calleja?

A Spanish mountaineer, adventurer and TV presenter from León who has climbed Everest and other 8,000 m peaks.

How high is Peña Ubiña?

2,417 m, the highest summit of the Ubiñas massif.

Where do you start the climb?

From Torrebarrio in Babia (León) or from Tuiza de Arriba in Lena (Asturias).

How far is it from Villablino?

Babia, where the León-side route starts, is the valley next to the Laciana Valley, so it is a short drive.
