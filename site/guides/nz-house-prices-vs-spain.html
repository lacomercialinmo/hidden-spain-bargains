Running costs

A yearly municipal tax (IBI), and, if you don't live in Spain, a yearly non-resident tax: 1.1% of the cadastral value (2% if it has not been revised in the last ten years), taxed at 19% for EU/EEA residents or 24% for everyone else. All costs explained.

What your money buys in Villablino

In Villablino, in León's Laciana Valley, homes cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026), against €2,429/m² (average, Q1 2026) for Spain as a whole. Our current listing is a 3-bedroom, 74 m² flat at €42,000, furnished and south-facing. See it here or read our full guide to Villablino.
