Remote work in Spain on a US salary

With the Spanish digital nomad visa you can live in Spain and keep working for a US company. You need an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 6 min read

The visa

You need an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients. You also need a degree or proven experience in your field, and a letter from your employer or contracts with your clients.

Time zones

Spain is six hours ahead of New York and nine ahead of California. Many remote workers in Spain work mornings for themselves and afternoons and evenings overlapping US hours.

Taxes

As a US citizen you keep filing US returns. If you live in Spain more than 183 days a year you also become a Spanish tax resident. Get cross-border advice before you move.

Frequently asked questions

Can I work for a US company while living in Spain?

Yes, with the digital nomad visa, if most of your income comes from outside Spain.

What income do I need?

An income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients.
