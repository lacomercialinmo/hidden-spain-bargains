Las Médulas and El Bierzo: landscapes that rival home

You grew up with some of the world's great landscapes, so you're hard to impress. Las Médulas might do it: a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997 and the largest open-pit gold mine of the Roman Empire, about 25 km south-west of Ponferrada.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

Las Médulas

Red peaks and tunnels carved by Roman engineers, rising from chestnut forests. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997 and the largest open-pit gold mine of the Roman Empire, about 25 km south-west of Ponferrada.

El Bierzo

A green basin of vineyards around Ponferrada, a city of 63,186 residents (INE, 2025). The Knights Templar held the town until 1312, and the castle covers about 8,000 m².

The Laciana Valley

North of Ponferrada, Villablino sits in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve with homes around €545/m² (median, October 2026).

Living here, not just visiting

Villablino sits at about 1,015 m in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 23,000 hectares, with trails from every village and Leitariegos ski resort about 15 km away. It is a real town of 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), with fibre internet and services, and homes around €545/m² (median, October 2026).
