Moving to Spain from the UK in 2026: the honest guide

Since Brexit, UK passport holders can stay up to 90 days in any 180-day period in the Schengen area without a visa. To live here you need a visa, but the routes are clear, and a home in Spain can cost a fraction of one in Britain.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer: remote workers use the digital nomad visa (an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients); retirees and savers use the non-lucrative visa (€28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026, which is 400% of the IPREM index, plus €7,200 for each family member, and you cannot work in Spain). State pensioners can get Spanish public healthcare through the S1 form.

Why so many Britons are looking at Spain

The average UK home costs £273,000 (UK average, July 2026) and the average private rent is £1,393 a month (UK average private rent, July 2026). Many people pay that rent for years and still can't buy. In much of rural Spain you can own a home outright for the price of a deposit in Britain.

The visa you need

  • Working for a UK employer or clients: the digital nomad visa. You need an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients.
  • Retired or living on savings: the non-lucrative visa. You need €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026, which is 400% of the IPREM index, plus €7,200 for each family member, and you cannot work in Spain.

Owning a home does not give you residence on its own. See our visa guide.

Healthcare and pensions

UK State Pension recipients living in Spain may get Spanish state healthcare paid by the UK through an S1 form. And the UK State Pension is paid in Spain and uprated every year.
