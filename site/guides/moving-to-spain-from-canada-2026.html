Moving to Spain from Canada: your three ways in

Canadians have one advantage most people don't: a working holiday visa open until age 35. After that, the digital nomad and non-lucrative visas cover most people.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 7 min read

Quick answer: under 36, the working holiday visa gives you up to 12 months of living and working in Spain (ages 18–30 for Australians and New Zealanders, 18–35 for Canadians). Remote workers use the digital nomad visa (an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients). Retirees use the non-lucrative visa (€28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026, which is 400% of the IPREM index, plus €7,200 for each family member, and you cannot work in Spain).

Why Canadians are looking at Spain

You have probably done the maths many times. The national average home in Canada sells for C$666,538 (national average sale price, September 2026), and in the Greater Toronto Area the average is C$1,024,108 (GTA average selling price, October 2026). The three routes

  • Working holiday (18–35): up to 12 months of living and working in Spain (ages 18–30 for Australians and New Zealanders, 18–35 for Canadians). Perfect for trying Spain before you buy.
  • Digital nomad visa: keep your Canadian job or clients. You need an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients.
  • Non-lucrative visa: for living on savings or a pension. You need €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026, which is 400% of the IPREM index, plus €7,200 for each family member, and you cannot work in Spain.

Frequently asked questions

Can Canadians work in Spain?

Yes, with the right visa. Canadians aged 18–35 can use the working holiday visa; others can use the digital nomad visa to work remotely for foreign clients.

What is the age limit for the Spanish working holiday visa for Canadians?

35.
