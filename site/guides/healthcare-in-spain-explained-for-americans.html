Healthcare in Spain, explained for Americans

Spain has a public health system for people who live and work here, and a large private sector. If you move on a visa, you will usually start with private insurance.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

Public healthcare

People who work and pay social security in Spain are covered by the public system. Pensioners from some countries are covered through agreements with their home country.

Private insurance for visa holders

Applicants for the non-lucrative visa need private health insurance valid in Spain. Many digital nomads also start with private cover.

What is near Villablino

The town has a health centre with an emergency service and pharmacies. The reference hospital is Hospital El Bierzo in Ponferrada, 62 km away.

A calm place with everything close by

Villablino has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), a health centre with an emergency service, pharmacies and shops within walking distance, and Hospital El Bierzo in Ponferrada as its reference hospital.
