From Toronto to Castellón: trading stress for the Mediterranean

You work hard, you pay a fortune and you still feel you're running to stand still. The average home in the Greater Toronto Area sells for C$1,024,108 (GTA average selling price, October 2026). On the Mediterranean coast of Castellón, homes average €1,443/m² (average, Q1 2026), the lowest of the three Valencian provinces, against €1,843 in Valencia and €2,214 in Alicante.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 6 min read

A different kind of rich

Some of the happiest people we know left big, expensive cities for smaller places where life is slower. Castellón is that kind of place: a Mediterranean province between Valencia and Barcelona, with beaches, orange groves, mountain villages inland and a calm provincial capital.

The weather you've been dreaming of in February

Castellón has January highs of about 14°C, August highs of about 31°C and around 444 mm of rain in a normal year. No snow shovelling, no ice on the windscreen.

How to get there legally

Under 36? The working holiday visa gives you up to 12 months of living and working in Spain (ages 18–30 for Australians and New Zealanders, 18–35 for Canadians). Otherwise, remote workers use the digital nomad visa (an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients) and savers the non-lucrative visa.
