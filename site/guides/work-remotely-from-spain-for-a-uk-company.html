Remote work

Working remotely from Spain for a UK company

The Spanish digital nomad visa lets you live in Spain while you work for a UK employer or clients. You need an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 6 min read

Who the visa is for

Employees of foreign companies and freelancers with foreign clients. Most of your work must come from outside Spain: an income of €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026, which is 200% of the Spanish minimum wage, and no more than 20% of the work for Spanish clients.

Time zone

Spain is one hour ahead of the UK all year, so your working day lines up almost exactly with your colleagues'.

Internet in the mountains

Villablino has fibre internet in town plus 4G and 5G. For homes in the smaller villages we check the connection at the exact address before you decide.

Taxes

If you live in Spain more than 183 days a year you normally become a Spanish tax resident. Talk to a cross-border tax adviser before you move. Read our tax overview.
