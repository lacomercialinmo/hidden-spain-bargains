Remote work

Work remotely from Spain for a Canadian company

Your job is already remote. The only thing tying you to an expensive city is habit. With the Spanish digital nomad visa you need €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026 (200% of the Spanish minimum wage), with no more than 20% of work for Spanish clients.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

The visa

You need €2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026 (200% of the Spanish minimum wage), with no more than 20% of work for Spanish clients, plus a degree or proven experience and a letter from your employer or contracts with clients.

Time zone

Spain is six hours ahead of Toronto. A common rhythm: your own time in the morning, Canadian meetings in the afternoon.

Internet

Villablino has fibre internet in town plus 4G and 5G.

Taxes

If you live in Spain more than 183 days a year you normally become a Spanish tax resident. Plan the move with a cross-border tax adviser.

Where we would start looking: Villablino

Villablino is a mountain town in León, north-west Spain, and the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares. It has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), sits at about 1,015 m above sea level, and has fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, supermarkets and banks. León Airport is 98 km away and Asturias Airport 153 km, and Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from town.

Homes here cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026), against €2,429/m² (average, Q1 2026) for Spain as a whole. See the homes we have selected or read our full guide to Villablino.
