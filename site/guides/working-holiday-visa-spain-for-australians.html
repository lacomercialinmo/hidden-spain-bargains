Spain's working holiday visa for Australians

If you are an Australian aged 18 to 30, Spain gives you up to 12 months for citizens of Australia and New Zealand aged 18–30 and Canada aged 18–35. It's the gap year that turns into a plan.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

The basics

The scheme gives up to 12 months for citizens of Australia and New Zealand aged 18–30 and Canada aged 18–35. You apply at the Spanish consulate in Australia and must show savings and insurance.

Use the year well

  • Learn Spanish properly.
  • Live in more than one region: the north is green and mountainous, the south dry and sunny.
  • Look at homes and talk to locals about prices.

When the year is up

To stay, move on to the digital nomad visa (€2,849 a month for a single applicant in 2026 (200% of the Spanish minimum wage), with no more than 20% of work for Spanish clients) or the non-lucrative visa.

Where we would start looking: Villablino

Villablino is a mountain town in León, north-west Spain, and the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares. It has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), sits at about 1,015 m above sea level, and has fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, supermarkets and banks. León Airport is 98 km away and Asturias Airport 153 km, and Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from town.

Homes here cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026), against €2,639/m² (second-hand homes, Q4 2025) for Spain as a whole. See the homes we have selected or read our full guide to Villablino.
