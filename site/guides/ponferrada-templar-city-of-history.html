Ponferrada: a Templar city for people who love history

Some places have stories older than whole countries. In Ponferrada, the Knights Templar built one of Spain's great castles. Pilgrims on the Camino de Santiago still walk past its walls.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 6 min read

A castle that is the city's heart

Ponferrada's castle is one of the great fortresses of north-west Spain. The Knights Templar held the town until 1312, and the castle covers about 8,000 m². You don't visit it once; you walk past it on your way to buy bread.

Roman gold and a wine valley

Las Médulas is a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1997 and the largest open-pit gold mine of the Roman Empire, about 25 km south-west of Ponferrada. Around Ponferrada, El Bierzo is a green valley of vineyards and orchards.

A real city, not a museum

Ponferrada has 63,186 residents (INE, 2024), the region's reference hospital (Hospital El Bierzo) and a train station: direct trains to Madrid take around four hours.

Mountains next door

An hour from Ponferrada, Villablino sits in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve with homes around €539/m² (median, October 2026). City life and mountain life, a short drive apart.
