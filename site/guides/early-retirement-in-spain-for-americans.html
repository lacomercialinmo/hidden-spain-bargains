Retirement

Early retirement in Spain for Americans

If you want to stop working and live on savings or a pension, the non-lucrative visa is the usual route. You need €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026 (400% of IPREM), plus €7,200 for each family member; no work allowed in Spain.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 6 min read

The non-lucrative visa

You need €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026 (400% of IPREM), plus €7,200 for each family member; no work allowed in Spain, private health insurance and a clean criminal record. You cannot work in Spain on this visa. After five years of legal residence you can apply for long-term residence.

Make your money last

The biggest cost in retirement is housing. With homes in Villablino at around €539/m² (median, October 2026), many retirees buy outright and live without rent or a mortgage.

Healthcare

Visa applicants need private health insurance. Villablino has a health centre with an emergency service, and the reference hospital for the area is Hospital El Bierzo in Ponferrada.

Where we would start looking: Villablino

Villablino is a mountain town in León, north-west Spain, and the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares. It has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), sits at about 1,015 m above sea level, and has fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, supermarkets and banks. León Airport is 98 km away and Asturias Airport 153 km, and Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from town.

Homes here cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026), against €2,639/m² (second-hand homes, Q4 2025) for Spain as a whole. See the homes we have selected or read our full guide to Villablino.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to retire in Spain?

For the non-lucrative visa: €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026 (400% of IPREM), plus €7,200 for each family member; no work allowed in Spain.

Can I work on a non-lucrative visa?

No. It is for people who live on their own means without working in Spain.
