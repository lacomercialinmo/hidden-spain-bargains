7 Spanish towns under €1,150 per m² with a direct train to Madrid

These seven Spanish towns have flats for under €1,150 per m², against €2,787/m² for flats in Spain as a whole, and every one has a direct train to Madrid. You get a real town with a station, shops and health services, not an empty village.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 6 min read

Quick answer: Bembibre, Puertollano, Linares, Arévalo, Ponferrada, Astorga and Medina del Campo. The fastest link is Medina del Campo, about 55 minutes on the fastest direct trains to Madrid Chamartín. The lowest prices are in Bembibre and Puertollano. Why a train to Madrid matters

Madrid-Barajas has direct flights to the UK, Ireland, North America and much of the world. With a direct train you can get from your front door to the capital without a car, see family abroad without a long drive, and reach Madrid's hospitals, embassies and consulates in a day.

Town by town

1. Bembibre (León, Castile and León)

A friendly market town in El Bierzo, about 20 minutes from Ponferrada, among vineyards and chestnut woods. Flats cost about €696/m². Train: one direct train a day to Madrid Chamartín, taking about 4 hours, which also stops at Bembibre and Astorga. Read our full guide to Bembibre.

2. Puertollano (Ciudad Real, Castilla-La Mancha)

A real town of 45,000 people on the Madrid–Seville high-speed line, with sunny central-Spain weather. Flats cost about €772/m². Train: about 1 h 10 min to 1 h 20 min by high-speed train, with more than 15 direct trains a day. Read our full guide to Puertollano.

3. Linares (Jaén, Andalusia)

A mining city with grand boom-era buildings, birthplace of the guitarist Andrés Segovia, close to UNESCO-listed Úbeda and Baeza. Flats cost about €928/m². Train: about 3 hours to Madrid Atocha, with around 5 direct trains a day from Linares-Baeza station, about 10 km from town. Read our full guide to Linares.

4. Arévalo (Ávila, Castile and León)

A brick Mudéjar town where Isabella of Castile grew up, between Ávila, Segovia and Valladolid. Flats cost about €1,052/m². Train: about 2 hours on direct regional trains to Madrid Príncipe Pío, around 7 a day. Read our full guide to Arévalo.

5. Ponferrada (León, Castile and León)

The Templar city of El Bierzo, with a hospital, a university campus and Las Médulas a short drive away. Flats cost about €1,071/m². Train: one direct train a day to Madrid Chamartín, taking about 4 hours, which also stops at Bembibre and Astorga. Read our full guide to Ponferrada.

6. Astorga (León, Castile and León)

A Roman town where two Camino routes meet, with an Episcopal Palace by Gaudí and a long chocolate tradition. Flats cost about €1,096/m². Train: one direct train a day to Madrid Chamartín, taking about 4 hours, which also stops at Bembibre and Astorga. Read our full guide to Astorga.

7. Medina del Campo (Valladolid, Castile and León)

A royal market town with a great brick castle, one of Spain's largest main squares and its own hospital. Flats cost about €1,137/m². Train: about 55 minutes on the fastest direct trains to Madrid Chamartín. Read our full guide to Medina del Campo.

What the low price does and does not mean

A price per m² is an average. In every one of these towns you will find flats that need work and others ready to move into. Before you buy, check the building, the community fees and the energy certificate. Frequently asked questions

Which Spanish town under €1,150 per m² is closest to Madrid by train?

Medina del Campo: about 55 minutes on the fastest direct trains to Madrid Chamartín.

Where are flat prices lowest in this list?

Bembibre, at about €696/m², followed by Puertollano at about €772/m².

Do these towns have hospitals?

Ponferrada, Linares, Puertollano and Medina del Campo have their own hospitals. Bembibre and Astorga use Hospital El Bierzo in Ponferrada and the hospitals in León; Arévalo has a health centre and uses the hospital in Ávila.
