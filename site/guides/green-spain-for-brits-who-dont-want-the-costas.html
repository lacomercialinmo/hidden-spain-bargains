Green Spain: for Britons who don't want the Costas

Not everyone dreams of a beach bar. North-west Spain is green, mountainous and quiet, more Lake District than Benidorm, but with more sun and much lower prices.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

What it is like

The Laciana Valley is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares, with peaks above 2,000 metres, birch forests and rivers. The weather has four real seasons: average highs of about 7°C in January and 28°C in July, with drier summers (approximate model averages).

Wildlife

The mountains around the valley are home to the Cantabrian brown bear and the capercaillie. They live in the mountains, not in town. Next door, Somiedo is an Asturian natural park and Biosphere Reserve (since 2000) of 283 km², a stronghold of the Cantabrian brown bear, with guided bear-watching trips.

Winter sports and walking

Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from Villablino, and trails start from the villages of the valley.

Living here, not just visiting

Villablino sits at about 1,015 m in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares, with trails from every village and Leitariegos ski resort about 15 km away. It is a real town of 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), with fibre internet and services, and homes around €539/m² (median, October 2026). Frequently asked questions

Is north-west Spain rainy?

It is greener and wetter than southern Spain, with drier summers: average highs of about 7°C in January and 28°C in July, with drier summers (approximate model averages).

Are there bears near Villablino?

Yes, in the surrounding mountains, not in town.
