From the edge of the world to the middle of Europe

From Australia, Europe is a 20-hour journey. From Spain, it's a weekend. Living here changes what a holiday means.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 4 min read

Connected, even from the mountains

Villablino is 98 km from León Airport and 153 km from Asturias Airport. From Ponferrada, direct trains to Madrid take around four hours, and Madrid's airport connects to the whole of Europe.

Same time zone as Paris and Rome

Spain runs on Central European Time, so calls with friends in London, Paris or Berlin are easy. Calls home need planning: eastern Australia is around eight to ten hours ahead of Spain, depending on the season.

A base that costs little

With homes in Villablino around €539/m² (median, October 2026), you can own your base in Europe outright and spend on travel instead of rent.

Where we would start looking: Villablino

Villablino is a mountain town in León, north-west Spain, and the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares. It has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), sits at about 1,015 m above sea level, and has fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, supermarkets and banks. León Airport is 98 km away and Asturias Airport 153 km, and Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km from town.

Homes here cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026), against €2,639/m² (second-hand homes, Q4 2025) for Spain as a whole. See the homes we have selected or read our full guide to Villablino.
