Brown bears and birch forests: the wild Spain

New Zealand's wildlife is famous for its birds. In the mountains of north-west Spain, you share the valleys with the Cantabrian brown bear.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

The bears of the Cantabrian Mountains

The Laciana Valley, around Villablino, is one of the strongholds of the Cantabrian brown bear and the capercaillie. The bears live in the mountains, not in town.

Where to watch them

Next door in Asturias, Somiedo is an Asturian natural park and Biosphere Reserve (since 2000) of 283 km², a stronghold of the Cantabrian brown bear, with guided bear-watching trips.

Forests you'll walk every weekend

The valley is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares, with peaks above 2,000 metres and some of the finest birch forests in the Iberian Peninsula.

Living here, not just visiting

Villablino sits at about 1,015 m in a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, about 22,000 hectares, with trails from every village and Leitariegos ski resort about 15 km away. It is a real town of 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), with fibre internet and services, and homes around €539/m² (median, October 2026).
