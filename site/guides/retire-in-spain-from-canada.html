Retiring to Spain from Canada

You built a life in Canada. Now you can choose where to enjoy it. Sell a home worth around C$666,538 (national average sale price, September 2026), buy one in Spain outright, and keep most of the difference.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

The visa

The non-lucrative visa is the retirement route. You need €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026, which is 400% of the IPREM index, plus €7,200 for each family member, and you cannot work in Spain and private health insurance.

The home

In Villablino homes cost around €539/m² (median, October 2026); in Castellón city, €1,317/m² (average, Q1 2026). Mountains or sea, the maths works.

Healthcare

Villablino has a health centre with an emergency service. The reference hospital is Hospital El Bierzo in Ponferrada.

A calm place with everything close by

Villablino has 7,585 residents (INE, 2025), a health centre with an emergency service, pharmacies and shops within walking distance, and Hospital El Bierzo in Ponferrada as its reference hospital. Frequently asked questions

What visa do I need to retire in Spain?

Usually the non-lucrative visa: €28,800 a year for the main applicant in 2026, which is 400% of the IPREM index, plus €7,200 for each family member, and you cannot work in Spain.

How much is a home in Spain?

In Villablino around €539/m² (median, October 2026); in Castellón city €1,317/m² (average, Q1 2026).
