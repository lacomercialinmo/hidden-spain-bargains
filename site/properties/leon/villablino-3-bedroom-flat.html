This is a 74 m² flat with three bedrooms, one bathroom and a living-dining room, on the second floor of a building in a very quiet part of Villablino. It faces south, so it gets natural light all day, and it is sold furnished, with heating.

Life in Villablino

Villablino is the main town of the Laciana Valley, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve since 2003, with 7,585 residents (INE, 2025). The town has fibre internet, a health centre with an emergency service, schools, supermarkets and banks. Leitariegos ski resort is about 15 km away, León Airport 98 km and Asturias Airport 153 km.
