How to buy a home in Spain from abroad

You can buy a home in Spain without being here for every step. You need a Spanish tax number (NIE), a way to see the home, and someone to sign for you at the notary if you can't come.

By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 5 min read

1. Get your NIE

The NIE is the identity number every foreigner needs to buy property in Spain. You can apply at a Spanish consulate in your country or in Spain. Without it, the notary cannot sign the sale.

2. See the home by video

We arrange a live video call with the owner so you can walk through every room, open windows and ask questions. We also send you the area guide, so you know the town before you visit.

3. Check the paperwork

Before you pay anything, the land registry extract (nota simple) shows who owns the home and whether it has debts or a mortgage. We ask for it and go through it with you.

4. Reserve and sign

  • Deposit contract (arras): you usually pay a deposit to take the home off the market. The contract says what happens if either side pulls out.
  • Notary: the sale is signed at a notary. The buyer can choose the notary.
  • Power of attorney: if you can't travel, you can give a power of attorney to a lawyer or person you trust. You sign it at a notary or Spanish consulate in your country, with an apostille where needed.

5. After the keys

The deed is registered at the land registry in your name, the taxes are paid, and you put the utilities in your name. We tell you who to call for each one.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to travel to Spain to buy?

No. You can view by video and sign through a power of attorney.

What is an NIE?

The tax identity number every foreigner needs to buy property in Spain.

Who chooses the notary?

In Spain the buyer normally has the right to choose the notary.
