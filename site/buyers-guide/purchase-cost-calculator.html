How much does it really cost to buy a home in Spain?

On a resale home in Spain you pay the price plus a regional transfer tax (from 6% in the Madrid region to 9% in Castilla-La Mancha and the Valencia region) plus notary and land registry fees, usually about €600 to €1,500 together, depending on the price. By La Comercial Inmobiliaria · Legal and tax figures checked November 2026 · 2 min read

What is included

  • Transfer tax (ITP): paid by the buyer on resale homes. Each region sets its own rate; the calculator uses the general rate for homes under €250,000.
  • Notary and land registry: regulated fees that depend on the price, usually about €600 to €1,500 together, depending on the price.
  • Lawyer or gestoría: optional, but most buyers from abroad use one. Ask for a written quote and add it above.

Not included: a mortgage (the bank pays most of its costs), moving, furniture and any work on the home. After you buy, you also pay a yearly local tax (IBI) and, if you live abroad, a yearly non-resident tax. See all the taxes explained.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax do I pay to buy a resale home in Spain?

A regional transfer tax: from 6% in the Madrid region to 9% in Castilla-La Mancha and the Valencia region at the general rate, plus notary and registry fees.

Is the tax lower in the Madrid region?

Yes. The general rate there is 6%, one of the lowest in Spain.

Which exchange rate does the calculator use?

The European Central Bank reference rate of the day. Your bank or transfer service will use its own rate.
