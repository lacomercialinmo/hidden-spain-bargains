# Deep research per town (sources: town halls, regional tourism, blogs). Checked 2026-11-15.

## Peñafiel
- Castle: 15th c., built by Pedro Girón (master of Order of Calatrava); owned by the town council, fully restored; houses the Wine Museum (Museo del Vino). [turismocastillayleon]
- Plaza del Coso: since Middle Ages for jousts, festivals, tournaments; current structure 18th c.; 48 houses with wooden balconies still the stage for fiestas (bulls) and Bajada del Ángel on Easter Sunday. Tourist office on the square. [turismocastillayleon, barcelo, hoteles.net]
- Iglesia de Santa María with Museo Comarcal de Arte Sacro; Convento de San Pablo holds remains of Don Juan Manuel, who wrote 'El Conde Lucanor' in the town. Torre del Reloj; Casa Museo de la Ribera (20th-c. rural life). [tuscasasrurales, escapadarural]
- Fiestas de Nuestra Señora y San Roque 14–18 Aug, Regional Tourist Interest; chupinazo starts 4 days. Carnival in Feb. [terranostrum, turismocyl, hoteles.net]
- Nearby: Curiel de Duero (two castles, 6 km), Monasterio de Santa María de Valbuena (12th c., Cistercian, seat of Las Edades del Hombre), Pintia (Vaccean city, necropolis Las Ruedas, near Padilla de Duero). Hiking along the Duero (Senda del Duero, Parque Pedro Burgueño). [barcelo, escapadarural]
- 2024 finalist (top 10) Capital del Turismo Rural. Valladolid 55 km. "cradle of Ribera del Duero". [escapadarural, tuscasasrurales]

## Bembibre
- Capital of the Bierzo Alto; 24 km from Ponferrada, 80 km León, A-6 exit 374, Renfe station in town. [festivalesdeespana]
- Old town on a small hill with castle ruins; cobbled streets with traditional houses. Iglesia de San Pedro (medieval, Plaza del Ayuntamiento); Santuario del Ecce Homo (17th c.). [hoteles.net]
- Fiestas del Cristo (patronales), mid-September (2026 programme 11–15 Sept, town hall). [aytobembibre.es]
- Festival Nacional de Exaltación del Botillo (52nd edition 2025), Interés Turístico Provincial. Templar tradition ('Imposición de la Capa Templaria a Don Álvaro Yáñez' – hero of El Señor de Bembibre). [viralagenda, diariodeleon]
- Museo del Alto Bierzo (Centro Cultural de la Villa, C/ Lope de Vega 3). Teatro Benevívere with year-round programme; Casa de las Culturas y Biblioteca. 9 villages (pedanías). [turismocyl, viralagenda]

## Astorga
- Capital of La Maragatería; crossroads of Camino Francés and Vía de la Plata. [viajecaminodesantiago]
- Plaza Mayor built over the Roman forum; 17th-c. baroque town hall with clock where two maragato figures (Juan Zacunda and Colasa) strike the hours. Casa Granell by Antonio Palacios. [barcelo]
- Cathedral (works from 15th c.): retablo by Gaspar Becerra, 16th-c. choir stalls, Inmaculada by Gregorio Fernández. Gaudí's Episcopal Palace houses Museo de los Caminos. [viajecamino, elviajedesofi]
- Roman walls: ~30 towers (cubos) survive. Museo Romano; Ruta Romana (book via tourist office). Museo del Chocolate; Casa Panero; street-art route. Iglesia de San Bartolomé = oldest religious building. [viajecamino, barcelo, tuscasasrurales]
- Fiestas: Astures y Romanos (summer), Santa Marta, Semana Santa. Food: cocido maragato (eaten 'backwards'), chocolate, mantecadas; tapas around Plaza Mayor at weekends. [minube, barcelo]

## Venta de Baños
- San Juan de Baños (Baños de Cerrato): ordered by Visigothic king Recesvinto in 661 over Roman remains; dedication inscription on the arch; eight Corinthian capitals; one of the oldest Christian churches in Spain. Guided visit ~20 min, €2. Fuente de Recesvinto (legend: king cured by the spring). Mozarabic liturgy at the fiesta. [hoteles.net, spain.info, terranostrum, festivalesdeespana]
- Fiesta de San Juan Bautista (around 24 June; Interés Turístico de Castilla y León): procession with dulzaineros to the basilica, bonfire on St John's night with chocolate. Santa Rosa de Lima patron fiestas 18–23 Aug. Noche de las Velas in summer. [turismocyl, festivalesdeespana, ventadebanos.es]
- On the Canal de Castilla (south branch) – walking/cycling route; Charles V passed through. Railway junction. Castillo de Ampudia 24 km. [turismocyl, ventadebanos.es, picuco]

## Palencia
- Cristo del Otero: Victorio Macho, 1931, about 20 m tall, among the largest Christ statues in the world; viewpoint; museum of the sculptor; VR experience; 45-min walk or city bus. [aytopalencia, palenciaturismo, viajeroscallejeros]
- Calle Mayor: ~900 m arcaded shopping street (Bardem filmed 'Calle Mayor', 1956, here). Tapas around Plaza Mayor and Calle Mayor; 'gambas a la gabardina' at El Casero. [aytopalencia, viajeroscallejeros, hoteles.net]
- Cathedral of San Antolín ('Bella Desconocida'): Visigothic crypt, works by Berruguete, El Greco, Juan de Flandes. [aytopalencia]
- Canal de Castilla dock (dársena) with Museo del Agua (free); 207 km canal, late 18th c., built for wheat; Las Gatillas aqueduct; Frómista locks nearby. Riverside walks on the Carrión; Isabel II park. Fiestas: San Antolín (Sept), Semana Santa. 'Quiet, green city', walkable. [aytopalencia, palenciaturismo, spainroutes]

## Zamora
- 20+ Romanesque churches; cathedral built in 23 years (12th c.) with distinctive stone-scale dome; Torre del Salvador 45 m. Plaza Mayor with San Juan de Puerta Nueva, Old (Plateresque) and New town halls, Merlú monument. [esdiario, rumbo, mochileando]
- Castle with keep, gate and moat in Parque del Castillo; Puente de Piedra over the Duero; Calle Balborraz; Casa del Cid; Palacio de los Momos; Museo de Zamora; Parador (Palacio de los Condes de Alba de Aliste). Modernist buildings too. Mercado de Abastos. [spain.info, viajecamino]
- Semana Santa: International Tourist Interest; Miserere on Holy Thursday night. Ruta de la Plata. 'Zamora no se ganó en una hora' (siege 1072). Food: queso zamorano, garbanzos de Fuentesaúco, ternera de Aliste; Toro wine nearby. [spain.info, esdiario]

## Ponferrada
- Templar castle: works begun c. 1178; >8,000 m²; walk the walls and towers; Torre de Moclín best views over the Sil; tourist office in the old stables beside it; illuminated at night. [senditur, terranostrum, elespanol]
- Basílica de la Encina (1573, Renaissance; tower 'Giralda del Bierzo'); legend: Templars found the Virgin inside a holm oak c. 1300. Fiestas de la Encina 8 Sept (+ 'Encinina' 9 Sept). Also book, wine, ceramics, folk, crafts fairs and medieval market. [senditur, hoteles.net, guiaalfa, pilgrim]
- Old town: Plaza del Ayuntamiento (terraces, menú del día), Torre del Reloj, Iglesia de San Andrés. Museums: Energy (ENE.Museo), Railway, Radio (Luis del Olmo born here). [barcelo]
- Food: botillo with cachelos; cecina; pimientos asados del Bierzo; DO Bierzo wines. Rain ~640 mm; autumn/winter fog. Nearby: Molinaseca, Peñalba de Santiago, San Pedro de Montes (7th c. origin), Las Médulas. [spain.info, hoteles.net, guiaalfa, kupi]

## Aranda de Duero
- ~7 km of underground cellars dug between 12th and 18th c. under the old town (up to ~135 cellars); visits via tourist office; Bodega de las Ánimas; theatrical tours. Plan of 1503 at Simancas shows them. [terranostrum, senditur, guias-viajar]
- Santa María la Real (1439–early 16th c.; unusual four naves; Isabelline façade); San Juan church with Museo de Arte Sacro; Casa de las Bolas (15th-c. palace, Isabella said to have stayed); Palacio de los Berdugo; Humilladero; Puente de las Tenerías (medieval); CIAVIN wine-architecture centre and tourist office in Plaza Mayor; Railway museum. [terranostrum, omviajes, losviajeros]
- Santuario Virgen de las Viñas (17th c.); patron fiestas around 8 Sept; Sonorama music festival (August). Lechazo asado; Jornadas del lechazo. Buses to Madrid. [senditur, minube, losviajeros]

## Soria
- San Juan de Duero (Hospitaller monastery; 12th-c. church; 13th-c. cloister with interlaced arches; National Monument 1882); Ermita de San Saturio on rocks above the Duero (late 17th c./1703; free entry); San Polo walk 'Machado's path'; Concatedral de San Pedro (cloister); Santo Domingo (Romanesque façade); castle ruins & Mirador de los Cuatro Vientos; Casa-Museo/Instituto Antonio Machado (taught here from 1907; married Leonor 1909); Leonor's grave. [turismosoria, guias-viajar, destinia]
- Fiestas de San Juan o de la Madre de Dios (from the Thursday after 24 June; tourist interest); San Saturio patron 2 Oct; Festival de las Ánimas (Bécquer legend, Nov); Semana Santa. Tren Campos de Castilla. [turismosoria, destinia, senditur]
- Food: torreznos de Soria, mantequilla de Soria, mushrooms (micología), truffle. Hosts International Cross-country. [sorianitelaimaginas, turismosoria]

## Ávila
- Walls: ~2,516 m of granite, 87 towers (cubos); walk the adarve (ticket; free slot Tues 14–16h per blog); Puerta del Alcázar; best view from Los Cuatro Postes viewpoint (illuminated at night). [lamaletainquieta, omviajes]
- Cathedral (apse built into the walls, 'piedra sangrante'); Basílica de San Vicente (Romanesque–Gothic, outside walls). [lamaletainquieta, mochileando]
- St Teresa (born Ávila 1515): Convento de Santa Teresa on her birthplace (church & relics free, museum €3), Monasterio de la Encarnación (€2), San José (first foundation). Fiestas de Santa Teresa around 15 Oct; Semana Santa. [lamaletainquieta, mochileando]
- Plaza de Santa Teresa ('Mercado Grande') and Plaza del Mercado Chico (town hall). Food: 'triada' judías del Barco, chuletón (avileña negra ibérica), yemas de Santa Teresa; patatas revolconas, trucha. Tourist train. [rumbo, spain.info, vozpopuli]

## Medina del Campo
- 'Villa de las Ferias y del Testamento'; Conjunto Histórico-Artístico; guided visits. [terranostrum]
- Castillo de la Mota: ordered mid-15th c. by Juan II and Enrique IV, finished by Catholic Monarchs 1483; one of the largest in CyL; later state prison and archive; keep, parade ground, chapel, 'queen's dressing room'; tower & underground shooting galleries via guided tour. [turismocyl, spain.info, barcelo]
- Plaza Mayor de la Hispanidad: one of the largest in Spain (~1.5 ha), where the great fairs were held; Casa del Peso (tourist office); Colegiata de San Antolín (balcón del Pópulo; Piedad by Juan de Juni); Palacio Real Testamentario (Isabella died 1504); Museo de las Ferias (unique in Spain); Reales Carnicerías; Palacio de los Dueñas; Balneario (only spa in the province). Ferias y Fiestas de San Antolín (Sept, with bulls). Sunday market. [terranostrum, minube, losviajeros, daremapp]
- Food: lechazo + DO Rueda whites; cocadas. [barcelo, turismocyl]

## Medina de Rioseco
- 'Ciudad de los Almirantes'; Conjunto Histórico-Artístico since 1965; 'City of the Four Cathedrals' (Santa María de Mediavilla with Benavente chapel – Juan de Juni; Santiago; San Francisco; Santa Cruz = Holy Week museum). [elespanol, turismocyl, agronews]
- Rúa/Calle Mayor with porticoes and old-fashioned family shops. [deia, losviajeros]
- Canal de Castilla: largest dock (dársena) on the canal; boat 'Antonio de Ulloa' trips on the Ramal de Campos; San Antonio flour mill museum (turbine, mills). Three Kings arrive by boat on 5 Jan (Tourist Interest CyL). [agronews, terranostrum, turismocyl]
- Semana Santa: International Tourist Interest; huge pasos 'El Longinos' and 'La Escalera'; museum since 2000. Patron fiestas/romería in Sept. Pastries: bollos de baño, pelusas, marinas, abisinios. [elespanol, turismocyl, terranostrum]

## Arévalo
- Between rivers Arevalillo and Adaja; Conjunto Histórico; capital of La Moraña; 'Ciudad de los Cinco Linajes'; one of the main centres of Castilian Mudéjar. Antiques trade tradition. [turismocyl, terranostrum]
- Castle (15th c., reformed under Catholic Monarchs as artillery fortress; never a royal residence; now museum incl. Museo del Cereal; €4, summer Fri–Sun). Plaza de la Villa with timber-and-brick porticoed houses and Mudéjar towers (San Martín's twin towers 'Torres Gemelas'); Santa María la Mayor; San Nicolás de Bari (Isabella's baptism font); Real Hospital de la Purísima (cultural centre & tourist office); Ermita de La Lugareja (2 km, summit of Romanesque-Mudéjar); medieval bridges. Proverb: 'Quien de Castilla señor quiera ser, a Olmedo y Arévalo de su parte ha de tener'. [turismocyl, guiarepsol, hola, spain.info]
- Food: Tostón de Arévalo (roast suckling pig, brand); white chickpeas; roast lamb/kid. Fiestas around 7 July (per hoteles.net – unverified exact). Pine woods & riverbanks. [turismocyl, hoteles.net]

## Nava de la Asunción
- Rises from a 'spectacular sea of pine forests' (Tierra de Pinares); Charles III granted it the title of villa. Jaime Gil de Biedma spent his childhood and youth here (reflected in his verses). Sights: parish church of Nuestra Señora de la Asunción, Bishop's Fountain, Casa de la Fuente, Ermita del Santo Cristo de la Expiración. On Camino de Santiago from Madrid. [turismocyl]
- Cycling: Tierra de Pinares cycle route (66 km), Vía Verde Valle del Eresma stages 2–3 pass Nava. Village of Moraleja de Coca belongs to it. [terranostrum, wikipedia]

## Laguna de Duero
- ~21,700 inhabitants; 704 m; just SE of Valladolid; historic lagoon at its heart; Paseo del Lago. Pine forests (Pinar de los Valles, Castillejo) with easy trails e.g. 'El Pinar y la Acequia' 8 km, 2 h, cyclable; Duero riverbank. Health centre recently enlarged (8 new consultation rooms, 2025). Tourist office C/ Real 6. Living Nativity scene (Belén viviente, Asoc. Aceña). [tuscasasrurales, oysterlist, terranostrum, saludcyl, turismocyl]

## San Esteban de Gormaz
- Medieval villa on the Duero, Conjunto Histórico-Artístico 1995; on Ruta de la Lana and Camino del Cid; 'cradle of Soria Romanesque'. ~3,000 inhabitants; 18 hamlets; D.O. Ribera del Duero. [terranostrum, turismocyl, cronista]
- San Miguel church (1081) – earliest porticoed Romanesque gallery in Castile; Nuestra Señora del Rivero (12th c.) on hill over the valley; castle ruins (Arab origin, 9th c.) with view to Gormaz caliphal fortress; medieval bridge; Plaza Mayor porticoed; ~300 traditional wine cellars dug into castle hill (now social 'merenderos'); Parque Temático del Románico (scale models); Ecomuseo Molino de los Ojos. Lechazo in wood oven. N-122 to Aranda & El Burgo de Osma; buses to Valladolid, Madrid, Zaragoza. Tourist office Plaza del Frontón. [terranostrum, senditur, sorianitelaimaginas folleto, turismocyl]

## Ferrol
- Arsenal: 'only intact Enlightenment-era military port in Europe', largest naval base of its time (turismo.gal). Barrio de la Magdalena: 18th-c. grid ('chocolate bar' layout), glass galleries; Jardines de Herrera viewpoint; Museo Naval (free); Castillo de San Felipe guarding the ría; Ruta del Modernismo (~20 buildings, Rodolfo Ucha); Puerto de Curuxeiras; Ruta de la construcción naval. [turismo.gal, guias-viajar, hoteles.net, minube]
- Beaches: Doniños (~2 km, surf, Blue Flag yearly per paxinasgalegas), Esmelle, San Xurxo, Santa Comba, A Fragata. Tourist office Praza de España. Ferrolterra coast up to Pantín (surf). [pilgrim, paxinasgalegas, dacoruna]

## Avilés
- Casco histórico Conjunto Histórico-Artístico since 27 May 1955; porticoed streets Galiana, Rivero, La Ferrería; Plaza de España (town hall); Sabugo fishermen's quarter; San Nicolás de Bari (13th c.); Palacio de Valdecarzana; Teatro Palacio Valdés; Parque Ferrera (Woody Allen filmed Vicky Cristina Barcelona); La Carriona cemetery; ría walk; Salinas beach nearby (bus). Centro Niemeyer – only Niemeyer work in Spain. Festival: Fiesta del Bollo (Easter). On Camino del Norte; 25 km Gijón, 27 km Oviedo; cider (espichas). Tourist office C/ Ruiz Gómez 21. [turismoasturias, telecable, viajecaminodesantiago, elviajedesofi, guias-viajar]

## Pontevedra
- Casco histórico Conjunto Histórico-Artístico since 1951; often called second-best preserved in Galicia after Santiago; pioneer in pedestrianising its centre. Plazas linked to old trades: Leña, Herrería, Teucro, Verdura. Santuario da Virxe Peregrina (1778, Monumento 1982, BIC 2011) – patron of the Camino Portugués; Basílica de Santa María a Maior (16th c., mariners' guild, Galician Gothic); Ruinas de Santo Domingo (Museo de Pontevedra); Convento de Santa Clara; Parador in Pazo Casa do Barón; Mercado by the river Lérez; Alameda; Illa das Esculturas. Fiestas de la Peregrina (August); Feira Franca (Sept). Name from 'Pontis Veteris' (old bridge). Tourist office near Peregrina. [elespanol 2026, spain.info, turismoriasbaixas, paxinasgalegas, mochileando, rumbo]

## Ourense
- 'City of water': As Burgas hot springs gush at ~65–67 °C in the old town (symbols: Burgas, Santo Cristo, Roman bridge); free riverside thermal pools along the Miño (Chavasqueira, Outariz/Burga de Canedo – largest & free per spain.info; Outariz indoor paid). Roman bridge (Ponte Vella, 1st c. origin, rebuilt 13th & 17th). Catedral de San Martín, Plaza Mayor, casco vello. Eight bridges incl. Puente del Milenio. Wines: Ribeiro, Valdeorras, Ribeira Sacra, Monterrei. Nearby: Allariz (20 min), Ribadavia, Ribeira Sacra canyons of the Sil, Celanova, Verín; Entroido carnivals of Laza, Verín, Xinzo. [turismo.gal, spain.info, viajeroscallejeros, barcelo, mochileando]

## Lugo
- Roman wall (late 3rd c., ~2.1–2.3 km, 71 towers) – UNESCO 2000, only Roman wall preserving its full circuit; walkable on top; twinned with Great Wall (2007). Cathedral (from 12th c.) – part of the UNESCO Camino del Norte/Primitivo listing 2015. Domus del Mitreo; Puerta Miñá; Plaza Mayor; tapas streets Rúa da Cruz, Rúa Nova (free tapa culture). Arde Lucus Roman festival in June (Interés Turístico Nacional), San Froilán in October. Camino Primitivo stage ~98 km to Santiago; Terras do Miño Biosphere Reserve; Parque do Miño riverside. San Simón smoked cheese, Ribeira Sacra wines. [turismo.gal, paxinasgalegas, cronista, hola, inspain, viajeroscallejeros, spainroutes]

## Tarancón
- 'La Noble Ciudad de Tarancón'; on the river Riánsares; capital of La Mancha Alta; 818 m; ~16,700 inhab (INE 2025), second-largest town in Cuenca province; nearer to Madrid than to Cuenca; A-3/A-40. [hoteles.net, hispanopedia]
- Palace of the Dukes of Riánsares (by royal architect Narciso Pascual y Colomer, also designer of the Congreso) – today the town hall, with a collection of handmade Don Quixote sculptures and visitable caves; built for Agustín Fernando Muñoz, the local guardsman who secretly married Queen María Cristina. Iglesia de la Asunción (16th c., BIC, plateresque altarpiece); Casa-palacio de los Parada (17th c., contemporary art museum & archive); Arco de la Malena; Santuario de Riánsares (5 km, on the river, Visigoth Reccared legend); Roman bridge. [tarancon.es PDF, hoteles.net, escapadarural, komoot]
- Fiestas Virgen de Riánsares 7–13 Sept, starting with the 'galopeo'; Semana Santa & Pasión Viviente (Interés Turístico Regional); sweets: bizcochos borrachos. Nearby: Uclés monastery (~1 h from Madrid), Segóbriga Roman park, Belinchón. Camino Natural del río Riánsares. [escapadarural, elespanol 2026]

## Perales de Tajuña
- Vega del Tajuña, SE Madrid region, ~40 km from Madrid. Risco de las Cuevas: ~50–60 artificial caves cut into a gypsum cliff over the Tajuña (Carpetanian/protohistoric troglodyte settlement), Monumento since 1931; interpretation centre (Comunidad de Madrid invested >€400k, 2016); guided visits by local archaeologists; walk ~1 km from cemetery car park. [comunidad.madrid, todosobremadrid, hoteles.net, hispanopedia]
- Vía Verde del Tajuña (old Arganda railway 'el tren de Arganda, que pita más que anda'): flat, paved, family-friendly; Perales–Ambite 46 km; links Arganda, Morata, Tielmes, Carabaña, Ambite; Cornicabra station & quarries; Jarama battle remains. Iglesia de Santa María del Castillo; Fuente de Mariblanca (C/ Mayor Baja); Ermita de San Sebastián (baroque). [komoot, casasrurales, viasverdes, todosobremadrid]

## Puertollano
- Between Campo de Calatrava (volcanic) and Sierra Morena; mining past + modern art. Fuente Agria (iron-rich, volcanic, sparkling 'agua agria'; records since 1575; declared BIC Monumento 22 Mar 2024 DOCM) on Paseo de San Gregorio (19th-c. botanical-garden promenade) with Casa de Baños (1850 spa building), Ermita de la Virgen de Gracia (15th c.). Museo de la Minería y el Carbonífero + Castillete Pozo Norte; Ruta minera. Iglesia de la Asunción; Museo Municipal Cristina García Rodero (antiguo ayuntamiento). Laguna Blanca. Renfe AVE station (Madrid–Seville line). [castillalamancha DOCM, hoteles.net, renfe, civitatis, komoot]

## Cartagena
- Founded 227 BC by Hasdrubal (Qart Hadasht), Roman Carthago Nova; 3,000 years of history; naval base & natural harbour. Roman Theatre (5–1 BC; restored by Rafael Moneo; most-visited monument in the Region of Murcia; museum); Barrio del Foro Romano / Molinete; Casa de la Fortuna (mosaics); Muralla Púnica (3rd c. BC, interpretation centre on Monte Aletes – described as the only Punic wall in Europe); Byzantine wall; Castillo de la Concepción; Museo Nacional de Arqueología Subacuática (ARQVA); Peral submarine; coastal batteries (Castillitos); modernist centre (Palacio Consistorial). Cartagineses y Romanos festival (2nd half Sept). Beaches: Calblanque regional park (undeveloped dunes), Cala Cortina, La Manga, Cabo de Palos, Mar de Cristal. Food: caldero, michirones, asiático coffee. [spain.info, barcelo, hoteles.net, directoalpaladar, viajeroscallejeros, guias-viajar]

## Murcia (city)
- ~half a million people, 7th-largest city in Spain; Arab origin; on the river Segura in its fertile huerta. Cathedral (Plaza Cardenal Belluga, baroque façade & tower, capilla de los Vélez; Monumento Nacional 1931) on site of great mosque; Palacio Episcopal (18th-c.); Real Casino (from 1847, façade 1902 by Pedro Cerdán; Arab patio, Pompeian hall; 'Real' 2009) on calle Trapería; Platería; Plaza de las Flores tapas; Mercado de Verónicas; Paseo del Malecón; Museo Salzillo (nativity scene); Teatro Romea. Bando de la Huerta (Easter Tuesday, Interés Turístico Internacional) + Entierro de la Sardina = Fiestas de Primavera; famous Semana Santa. [spain.info, barcelo, guias-viajar, movelia]

## Xàtiva
- 60 km from Valencia; 'Ciudad de las Mil Fuentes' (~750 fountains; Trinitat fountain 14th-c. Gothic; 25 Caños neoclassical; Ruta de las Fuentes from tourist office/Font del Lleó); 'Cuna de los Papas' – birthplace of Borgia popes Calixtus III & Alexander VI (statues outside the Colegiata). Castle (double fortress Castell Menor & Major, BIC; tourist train), Colegiata/La Seu, Hospital Reial (founded 1244 by Jaime I), Sant Feliu (one of oldest churches in Kingdom of Valencia, 15th-c. altarpiece), Museo de l'Almodí (Ribera, Goya's Caprichos; Felipe V portrait hung upside-down), Jardines de Ibn Hazm (poet of 'The Ring of the Dove'), Plaza del Mercat. Fira d'Agost (August fair). Municipio Turístico CV since 2023. [barcelo, hoteles.net, comunitatvalenciana, viajeroscallejeros, uv.es, elespanol]

## Ontinyent
- Vall d'Albaida, on the river Clariano; town climbs like a staircase to walled Barrio de la Vila (Conjunto Histórico-Artístico, 1970s; documented 11th c.; Vila Reial after 1244). Palau de la Vila (Museu del Tèxtil Valencià, Gegants i Cabets museum, Tourist Info); Santa Maria church (14th c.) with bell tower said to be tallest in the Comunitat Valenciana (Ribalta, Benlliure works); Torre de l'Atxa (13th c.), lavadero, Plaza Major, Pont Vell, Palacio de la Duquesa de Almodóvar; Civil War air-raid shelters; Covetes dels Moros nearby (Bocairent). Pou Clar: six crystal river pools (Pou dels Esclaus… Pou dels Cavalls), 2 km from town, in Paraje Natural Municipal Serra de l'Ombria, summer access regulated with bus from polideportivo. Serra de Mariola natural park nearby. Moros y Cristianos (Interés Turístico Nacional, late Aug, for Santísimo Cristo de la Agonía), Fira, Corpus, Semana Santa. Food: sweets, oil, honey, embutido, rice. [comunitatvalenciana, elespanol 2026, hoteles.net, okdiario, komoot]

## Elda
- Middle Vinalopó valley, Alicante; ringed by mountains incl. 'La Cadira del Cid'; A-31 to Alicante & Madrid. 'Ciudad del Calzado' – shoe industry since late 19th c.; Museo del Calzado José María Amat Amer (Av. Chapí 32; described as one of the largest footwear collections in the world; celebrities' shoes). Castillo-Palacio (Almohad, 12th–13th c.); Iglesia de Santa Ana; Museo Arqueológico (Iberian 'Sirena de El Monastil'); El Monastil site; Monumento al Zapatero; Plaza Castelar. Moros y Cristianos (Interés Turístico Nacional, late May/June, patron San Antón), Fallas in March, Semana Santa, fiestas patronales Sept. Tourist Info C/ Nueva 14. [turismo.elda.es, comunitatvalenciana, planomato, hoteles.net]

## Badajoz
- 6 km from Portugal, on the Guadiana. Alcazaba (12th-c. Almohad citadel, one of the largest in Spain; Torre de Espantaperros; Puerta del Capitel; Museo Arqueológico Provincial in Palacio de los Condes de Roca; gardens). Plaza Alta (15th–17th c. arcades, red-and-white geometric façades, former souk) & Plaza de San José with Casas Mudéjares (tourist office, Plaza San José 18). Catedral de San Juan Bautista; Plaza de la Soledad with 'La Giralda' building; Plaza de España; Puerta de Palmas (early 16th c., Carlos V shield) and Puente de Palmas (16th c., ~600 m, 32 arches) to Fuerte de San Cristóbal; 17th-c. bastioned wall (one of most extensive in Spain per eldebate). Five bridges incl. modern Puente Real; tapas & Extremaduran ham/cheese; BIBA public bikes. [turismo.aytobadajoz.es, spain.info, renfe, viajeroscallejeros, eldebate 2026]

## Mérida
- Roman Augusta Emerita, capital of Lusitania; Conjunto Arqueológico UNESCO 1993. Roman Theatre (16–15 BC, Agrippa; ~6,000 seats; still used – Festival Internacional de Teatro Clásico, July–Aug, oldest/most important classical-theatre festival in Spain); Amphitheatre (same ticket); Templo de Diana; Puente Romano over the Guadiana (Anas); Acueducto de Los Milagros; Circo Romano; Casa del Mitreo; Arco de Trajano; Alcazaba (Moorish, unique aljibe of Roman/Visigoth stones); Basílica de Santa Eulalia (crypt found 1990); Museo Nacional de Arte Romano (Moneo); Proserpina Roman reservoir 5 km (beach area). Emerita Lvdica Roman festival (June). Joint ticket ~€12 (hoteles.net 2017 – re-check). ~58,000 people; regional capital of Extremadura. [spain.info, civitatis 2026, hoteles.net, eldiario, radiointerior]

## Almendralejo
- Capital of Tierra de Barros (red clay soils, vines & olives), near Mérida. 'Ciudad Internacional del Vino' and the only Extremaduran town in the D.O. Cava; D.O. Ribera del Guadiana. Museo de las Ciencias del Vino (in former alcohol factory with chimney, C/ Ciudad de Rubí); Museo del Cava (opened March 2025, described as Spain's only museum dedicated solely to cava, by Marcelino Díaz); neo-Mudéjar 19th-c. bullring with a winery under its stands (said to be unique). Iglesia de la Purificación (ZEPA for lesser kestrel), Ermita de la Piedad; 'Ciudad del Romanticismo' – birthplace of José de Espronceda & Carolina Coronado (Ruta del Romanticismo). Food: migas de Tierra de Barros, caldereta, sopa de tomate con uvas, cojondongo. Fiestas: Candelas (Feb bonfires), Romería de San Marcos (25 Apr), wine harvest fair. Ruta del Vino y Cava Ribera del Guadiana (since 2013; >24,000 visitors 2024). [hoteles.net, escapadarural, vinetur 2026, juntaex, cronista]

## Lepe
- Huelva, Costa de la Luz west; La Antilla beach (>3 km, fishing boats on the sand, 'Q de calidad', Ecoplayas, universal accessibility cert.), Islantilla (shared with Isla Cristina), Nueva Umbría (wild beach inside the Paraje Natural). Paraje Natural Marismas del Río Piedras y Flecha del Rompido (Lepe & Cartaya; free access, boardwalk trails La Turbera, Marismas de San Miguel, Nueva Umbría; birdwatching – spoonbills, avocets); boat trips from El Terrón fishing port; Torre del Catalán (16th-c. watchtower); Ermita de Nuestra Señora de la Bella; Parque de los Cabezos. Strawberries & citrus agriculture; ~20 min to Portugal border at Ayamonte. [barcelo, rumbo, hispanopedia, masmar, komoot, hola]

## Huelva (city)
- ~145,000 people; estuary of the Tinto & Odiel; Tartessian/Phoenician/Roman roots; most old buildings lost in 1755 Lisbon earthquake. British Rio Tinto Company legacy: Muelle del Tinto (19th-c. iron ore pier on the Odiel, BIC, restored 2003, famous sunsets); Barrio Reina Victoria / Barrio Obrero (English-style workers' quarter, BIC 1977); Spain's first golf course & early tennis courts; Casa Colón (built 1881–83 as luxury hotel, now home of the Ibero-American Film Festival); Gran Teatro; neo-Mudéjar Estación de Sevilla. Catedral de la Merced; Santuario de la Cinta (patron, views over marshes); Monumento a la Fe Descubridora (Columbus) at Punta del Sebo. Lugares Colombinos: La Rábida monastery, Muelle de las Carabelas (caravel replicas), Palos, Moguer. Marismas del Odiel (Paraje Natural 1983, Ramsar, UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, flamingos), Paseo de la Ría (2016), canoe boat to Punta Umbría beaches (summer); Mazagón, Doñana nearby. Food: gamba blanca de Huelva, jamón de Jabugo. [guiarepsol, andalucia.org, andalucia.com, barcelo, elcorteingles, spainroutes]

## Jerez de la Frontera
- Larger than the provincial capital Cádiz; 'Sherry, horses and flamenco'. Sherry bodegas (Tío Pepe/González Byass, Fundador, Garvey 1780, El Maestro Sierra; tours ~€18–35); Ruta del Vino y Brandy del Marco de Jerez. Real Escuela Andaluza del Arte Ecuestre ('Cómo bailan los caballos andaluces'; book ahead). Alcázar (12th-c. Almohad; mosque, baths, octagonal tower, cámara oscura in Torre de Villavicencio); Cathedral of San Salvador (finished late 17th c.); Barrio de Santiago (gitano cradle of bulerías). Feria del Caballo (early May, one week), Festival de Jerez flamenco (Feb–Mar, most international flamenco festival), Fiestas de la Vendimia (Sept); Circuito de Jerez MotoGP; zoo-botanical garden (>60,000 m²). [hoteles.net, barcelo, spanish-fiestas, triptamba]

## Córdoba
- Only city in the world with four UNESCO listings: Mezquita-Catedral (1984), historic centre (1994), Fiesta de los Patios (Intangible, 2012, May), Medina Azahara caliphal city (2018; founded 936 by Abd al-Rahman III, ~8–9 km away, shuttle bus, ~10% excavated). Also Roman bridge, Alcázar de los Reyes Cristianos (14th c.), Judería, Calleja de las Flores, Plaza de la Corredera, Palacio de Viana (patios), Templo Romano; Ruta de las Iglesias Fernandinas (11 churches). Food: salmorejo, flamenquín, rabo de toro. Cruces de Mayo, Patios, Feria de Mayo (Nuestra Señora de la Salud). AVE ~1h45 to Madrid. [hosteltur, eldiario, kayak, turismodecordoba.org, spain.info]

## Peñarroya-Pueblonuevo
- Valle del Guadiato, NW Córdoba province, ~76–79 km from Córdoba; two towns merged in 1927; early-20th-c. first coal-producing area of Spain (French Société Minière et Métallurgique de Peñarroya – SMMP), French influence in houses. Cerco Industrial (old foundry/industrial precinct) – BIC Lugar de Interés Industrial (2017/2018, first in Córdoba province); MITECO announced €4 million restoration (July 2026, CIUDEN + town hall + Asoc. La Maquinilla). La Yutera (design attributed to Eiffel's office) houses the Museo Geológico-Minero; IGME Litoteca; Plaza de Santa Bárbara; churches San Bernardino, San Miguel, Nuestra Señora del Rosario. Nearby: Belmez castle, Ruta de los Castilletes Metálicos (Fuente Obejuna–Espiel), dolmens; Vía Verde. [tuscasasrurales, dialnet, miteco 2026, BOJA 2018, viasverdes]

## Jaén (city)
- 'World capital of olive oil' surrounded by olive groves; capital of the 'Santo Reino'. Castillo de Santa Catalina on the city's highest peak (alcazaba origin 9th c.; conquered by Fernando III 1246) beside a Parador; Mirador de la Cruz sunsets. Cathedral of the Asunción (Renaissance landmark by Andrés de Vandelvira, candidate for UNESCO); Baños Árabes (11th c.) under Palacio de Villardompardo – largest/best-preserved Arab baths in Spain/Europe (Monumento Nacional), plus Museo de Artes y Costumbres Populares and Museo Internacional de Arte Naïf; Museo Íbero; Judería; Raudal de la Magdalena (fuente del Lagarto legend); Camino Mozárabe passes. The Guardian called it Spain's best-kept secret (eldebate Nov 2025). Tapas culture (free tapa). [hoteles.net, inspain, mochileando, hola, eldebate, renfe, gronze]

## Linares
- Iberian Cástulo (capital of the Oretani, settled since 3rd millennium BC) 5–7 km south – one of the most important archaeological sites of the Alto Guadalquivir (Paisaje de Interés Cultural minero Cástulo-Linares); Museo Arqueológico Monográfico de Cástulo in Palacio Dávalos Biedma (16th c., C/ General Echagüe). Mining boom (lead/silver; Casa de la Munición y Moneda under Carlos III; Centro de Interpretación del Paisaje Minero; old Estación de Madrid). Birthplace of guitarist Andrés Segovia (Casa-Museo in 17th-c. Palacio de los Orozco, tomb in crypt since 2002; town-hall clock plays his 'Estudio sin luz'; Encuentro Internacional de Guitarra) and singer Raphael (museum in El Pósito, 400+ pieces, statue); also Carmen Linares, Palomo Linares. Basílica de Santa María la Mayor, Hospital de los Marqueses, Palacio de Zambrana, Plaza de la Constitución. City title 1875; Linares-Baeza station; Vía Verde to Almería line. [andalucia.org, andalucia.com, hoteles.net, inspain, spain.info]

## Úbeda
- UNESCO World Heritage 2003 jointly with Baeza (10 km away) for its Renaissance ensemble (Francisco de los Cobos, secretary to Carlos V, and architect Andrés de Vandelvira). Plaza Vázquez de Molina: Sacra Capilla del Salvador (designed by Diego de Siloé, finished by Vandelvira; plateresque portal; entry ~€4–5), Palacio de las Cadenas/Vázquez de Molina (town hall since 1850, free visit), Palacio del Deán Ortega (Parador), Palacio del Marqués de Mancera, Basílica de Santa María de los Reales Alcázares, Antiguo Pósito, Venetian-style fountain. Also Hospital de Santiago, Iglesia de San Pablo, Sinagoga del Agua, Puerta de Santa Lucía viewpoint over the 'sea of olives' (Machado). Pottery quarter (alfarería, green glaze). Semana Santa Interés Turístico Nacional. Singer Joaquín Sabina grew up here. [turismodeubeda.com 2026, juntadeandalucia, civitatis, inspain, hoteles.net, elespanol 2026]

## La Carolina
- North Jaén on the A-4, last sizeable town before the Despeñaperros gorge ('gateway to Andalusia'). Founded 1767 by Carlos III under Pablo de Olavide as capital of the Nuevas Poblaciones de Sierra Morena (Enlightenment colonisation with central-European settlers, grid plan); grew around the Carmelite convent of La Peñuela (now Parroquia de la Inmaculada Concepción). Palacio del Intendente Olavide (neoclassical, royal coat of arms; town hall and Univ. of Jaén summer courses); Museo / Centro de Interpretación de la Minería y las Nuevas Poblaciones (lead mining since Roman times, minerals); Antigua Cárcel (Riego held 1823); monument to the Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa (1212; inaugurated 1881); Castillo de las Navas de Tolosa (BIC). Parque Natural de Despeñaperros 10 km north (gorges, forests, trails). Buses to Jaén, Linares, Úbeda, Valdepeñas. [andalucia.com, upo.es, directoalpaladar, ecured, visit-andalucia]
